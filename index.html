Privacy Policy

Meta Lead Monitor is a proof-of-concept application created for testing Meta Lead Ads integration.

Information We Collect

When a test lead is submitted through a Meta Lead Ad form, the application may receive information such as the lead's name, email address, and phone number.

How We Use Information

The information received from test leads is used only to demonstrate and test the Meta Lead Ads webhook integration.

Data Storage

This proof-of-concept does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of lead information. Test information is used only for demonstration and integration testing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share collected lead information with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the project owner through GitHub.
